# Design

Retain the existing oversized MONTAGE wordmark, warm paper, acid accent, dark workflow stage, and editorial spacing. Extend the page with a dark evidence section, a restrained beta offer card, and native disclosure-based FAQ. The first viewport stays outcome-led; proof appears before pricing. Every conversion link resolves to `/sign-in` so the page does not advertise a nonexistent sign-up or waitlist workflow.

Motion remains illustrative and respects reduced-motion preferences. Product truth comes from explicit verified capability copy and executable route/build tests, not the illustration.
