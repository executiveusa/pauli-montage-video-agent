# Stage checklist

- [ ] Inputs are identified by stable StudioProject/artifact IDs.
- [ ] Canon, rights, consent, privacy, safety, and budget constraints are loaded.
- [ ] Approved upstream decisions are referenced rather than re-decided silently.
- [ ] Work is limited to this stage's declared objective.
- [ ] Outputs are written under `output/` or canonical project storage.
- [ ] No source asset is overwritten.
- [ ] Verification evidence is recorded before handoff.
- [ ] `handoff.json` is updated with output refs, decisions, blockers, and next-stage context.
